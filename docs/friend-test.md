# CrashLab friend test

A pilot for Chapters 1 and 2 (Drive, Neuron, Read the brain, What neurons notice, Weights). With 3 to 5 people this shows where the app confuses them. It does not prove the app teaches.

## Before you start

- [ ] The live Netlify link works, including training (roadmap item F01).
- [ ] Testers have little or no ML background, and at least one uses a phone.
- [ ] Each tester agrees to take part and knows they can stop at any time.
- [ ] Use anonymous IDs (T1, T2, ...). Do not record names, numbers or email addresses.

## How to run a session (about 25 minutes)

1. Say: "This tests the app, not you. Please think out loud."
2. Give the quiz below, without help. Note the score.
3. Send the link. Say only: "Go as far as you can until 'Set three weights' is finished."
4. Stay quiet and take notes. Do not explain or point. If someone is stuck for 2 minutes, write it down, then give the smallest hint you can.
5. Give the same quiz again.
6. Ask the three questions under "Afterwards".

## Quiz

Give the same five questions before and after. Do not show the answers.

**1. What does a neuron do with its inputs?**
- A. Stores them for later
- B. Multiplies each by a weight, adds them up, and squashes the result
- C. Picks the largest one
- D. Sends them on unchanged

**2. A weight of 0 on a connection means:**
- A. The neuron is broken
- B. That input gets stronger
- C. The neuron ignores that input
- D. The output is always 0

**3. What decides what a neuron "notices"?**
- A. Its weights
- B. Its name
- C. The colour of the track
- D. Nothing, it is random each moment

**4. A car's right-side sensors stop working, and its brain treats that side as wide open. What is most likely?**
- A. It drives better
- B. Nothing changes
- C. It crashes because it cannot see that wall
- D. It grows new sensors

**5. A bank's model decides who gets a loan, but it is never given anyone's income. What follows?** (not about cars)
- A. It will work out income perfectly anyway
- B. It cannot use income, because it only knows its inputs
- C. It will refuse every loan
- D. It will ask the applicant

**Answers (keep hidden):** 1 B, 2 C, 3 A, 4 C, 5 B.

## Afterwards

1. What was the most confusing moment?
2. In your own words, what does a neuron do?
3. Would you send this to a friend? Why or why not?

## Reading the results

- Report raw scores only, and call it a pilot. Do not claim statistical significance.
- Repeating the same quiz can raise scores by itself, so a small rise proves little.
- Testers were chosen by you and may be polite, so trust what they did over what they say.
- The most useful result is where people got stuck. The same stuck point from two people is worth fixing first.
- These are your friends, so a good score does not stand for all users.
